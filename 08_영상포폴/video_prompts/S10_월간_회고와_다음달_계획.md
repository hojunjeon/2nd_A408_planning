# S10 — 월간 회고 → 다음 달 계획 | 8초 영상 생성 프롬프트



```text
@M5_keyfin_avatar
@M2_ai_cat
@M4_keyfin_room
@KeyFin_S10_reference

REFERENCE MAP

REF-A = M5_keyfin_avatar
REF-B = M2_ai_cat
REF-C = M4_keyfin_room
REF-D = KeyFin_S10_reference

Create ONE continuous 8-second stylized 3D KeyFin-world video in 16:9.

Maintain the exact stylized 3D visual language of REF-C.
Preserve the exact identities of REF-A and REF-B throughout all three shots.

CASTING LOCK:
ONLY M5 and M2 may appear.
NO real-world Yoon.
NO realistic human.
NO third character.
NO human hand from outside the scene.

CORE STORY:
SHOT A = M5 and M2 review the END-OF-MONTH spending results and analysis TOGETHER.
SHOT B = M2 AI coach proposes specific budget adjustments for NEXT MONTH.
SHOT C = M5 reviews the proposal and gives the FINAL APPROVAL.

These three shots must have clearly different UI states and clearly different actions.
Do NOT reuse the same chart or same interaction for all three shots.

TIMING:
SHOT A = 0.0–2.5 seconds
SHOT B = 2.5–5.2 seconds
SHOT C = 5.2–8.0 seconds

==================================================
[0.0–2.5s]
SHOT A — M5 + M2 REVIEW MONTH-END SPENDING TOGETHER
==================================================

PURPOSE:
This shot is MONTH-END REVIEW.
It is NOT yet the next-month proposal.
It is NOT approval.

START FRAME

M5 and M2 are already together in the KeyFin Room.

Use a comfortable seated or standing two-shot.
Both characters face one clean MONTH-END REPORT panel.

CAMERA

Stable medium-wide two-shot.

Use only a very subtle push-in.

Do not center the UI so heavily that the characters disappear.
M5 and M2 must both remain clearly visible.

MONTH-END REPORT UI

Show ONE clear monthly result panel containing:

- one simple monthly spending bar chart
- one simple category summary / donut chart
- 4 category rows maximum
- categories visually recognizable as:
  housing / living
  food
  transportation
  shopping

The UI should communicate:
“This is what happened this month.”

Do NOT show:
- next-month sliders
- confirmation check
- approval button
- proposed budget changes
- future budget arrows

M5 ACTION

M5 studies the monthly report.

Use:
- calm attentive expression
- small thoughtful head movement
- eyes following the chart

M2 ACTION

M2 reviews the SAME report together with M5.

M2 may glance between:
- the report
- M5

M2 can make one small analytical paw gesture,
but must NOT present the next-month solution yet.

IMPORTANT:

The visual relationship must feel like:
“M5 and M2 are reviewing this month together.”

Not:
“M2 is lecturing M5.”

END FRAME

Hold both M5 and M2 looking at the finished month-end analysis.

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[2.5–5.2s]
SHOT B — M2 PROPOSES NEXT-MONTH BUDGET ADJUSTMENTS
==================================================

PURPOSE:
This shot is the AI COACH'S PROPOSAL.

The UI must visibly CHANGE from the month-end report in SHOT A.

Do NOT reuse the same monthly chart as the primary UI.

START FRAME

M2 becomes slightly more visually prominent.

M5 remains present and listens.

CAMERA

Stable medium two-shot or over-M5-shoulder composition,
with M2 and the new proposal panel clearly readable.

NEXT-MONTH PROPOSAL UI

Replace the month-end report with a clean NEXT-MONTH BUDGET PROPOSAL panel.

Show 4 category budget rows maximum.

For each category,
show a simple current/reference level and a proposed next-month level.

The proposal should visually suggest small realistic adjustments such as:

- housing / fixed cost: unchanged
- food: slightly reduced or adjusted
- transportation: unchanged or slightly adjusted
- shopping: reduced more clearly

Use visual devices such as:
- paired bars
- small directional arrows
- slider positions
- subtle up/down indicators

IMPORTANT:

The viewer must immediately understand:
“M2 is proposing how to change next month's budget.”

Do NOT rely on long readable text.

Do NOT use a generic rising investment graph.

Do NOT use a stock-market style upward chart.

Do NOT show only historical analysis again.

M2 ACTION

M2 acts as the AI financial coach.

M2 gently points to ONE or TWO proposed changes.

For example:
- point to shopping budget being reduced
- then indicate food or living budget adjustment

Keep the movement calm and professional.

M2 should NOT:
- talk with exaggerated mouth movement
- dance
- celebrate
- wave wildly

M5 ACTION

M5 watches the proposal.

M5 should NOT approve yet.

M5 may:
- look from one proposed row to another
- show a thoughtful expression
- give a very small considering nod

But NO confirmation gesture yet.

END FRAME

Hold the clear NEXT-MONTH PROPOSAL state.

The viewer must understand:
“M2 analyzed this month and is now suggesting a revised plan for next month.”

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[5.2–8.0s]
SHOT C — M5 REVIEWS AND GIVES FINAL APPROVAL
==================================================

PURPOSE:
This shot is USER CONTROL / FINAL APPROVAL.

M2 has already made the proposal.
Now M5 makes the final decision.

START FRAME

Keep the same proposed next-month budget from SHOT B.

Do NOT introduce another new analysis.

Do NOT change the proposal before M5 acts.

CAMERA

Stable medium composition focused slightly more on M5 and the proposal controls.

Keep M2 visible but secondary.

M5 ACTION — REQUIRED ORDER

1. M5 looks over the proposed category adjustments.
2. M5 pauses briefly as if reviewing.
3. M5 performs ONE clear final approval action:
   - tap a confirm/check control
   OR
   - press one clear approval button area
4. ONLY AFTER the M5 approval,
   the proposal changes into a confirmed state.

UI RESPONSE AFTER APPROVAL

After M5 confirms:

- the proposed bars settle into final positions
- one clear green check appears
- the interface becomes visually stable
- optional subtle highlight indicates “plan confirmed”

Use only one confirmation event.

Do NOT let M2 confirm the plan.

Do NOT auto-confirm before M5 touches the control.

M2 ACTION

M2 watches M5 approve.

After approval,
M2 may show one small satisfied reaction:
- gentle closed-mouth smile
- tiny approving paw motion

Do not make M2 the decision-maker.

M5 EMOTION

M5 looks satisfied and confident.

Use a restrained expression.

No cheering.
No fist pumping.
No jumping.
No exaggerated open-mouth laughter.

END FRAME

Hold the final confirmed NEXT-MONTH BUDGET plan
with M5 as the clear final decision-maker
and M2 beside him as the supporting AI coach.

==================================================
UI STATE LOCK — CRITICAL
==================================================

SHOT A UI:
MONTH-END RESULT / ANALYSIS ONLY.

SHOT B UI:
NEXT-MONTH BUDGET ADJUSTMENT PROPOSAL ONLY.

SHOT C UI:
SAME PROPOSAL → M5 APPROVES → CONFIRMED PLAN.

The three states must be visually distinct.

DO NOT use the same generic chart in all three shots.

DO NOT show a continuously rising line graph as the main visual.

DO NOT make S10 look like investment performance analysis.

This scene is personal spending review and budget planning.

==================================================
CHARACTER ROLE LOCK
==================================================

M5:
- user
- reviews information
- considers proposal
- makes final approval

M2:
- AI financial coach
- analyzes
- proposes adjustment
- supports decision
- does NOT make the final decision

The story is WRONG if M2 approves for M5.

==================================================
STRICT FAILURE CONDITIONS
==================================================

The result is WRONG if:

- SHOT A shows only M5 or only M2
- M5 and M2 are not reviewing the month together
- SHOT A immediately shows next-month sliders instead of month-end results

- SHOT B repeats the same month-end chart from SHOT A
- SHOT B shows only a generic upward trend chart
- SHOT B does not clearly show proposed category-budget adjustments
- M5 approves during SHOT B

- SHOT C introduces a different proposal
- the plan auto-confirms before M5 acts
- M2 performs the final confirmation
- more than one confirmation occurs
- M5 does not visibly perform the approval

- real-world Yoon appears
- a third character appears
- M5 identity changes
- M2 identity changes
- the room changes
- the rendering becomes photorealistic
- dense unreadable text dominates the UI
- captions or subtitles appear

==================================================
EXACT STORY RHYTHM
==================================================

0.0–2.5s:
M5 and M2 TOGETHER review this month's spending results and analysis.

2.5–5.2s:
M2 AI coach presents a clearly different NEXT-MONTH budget adjustment proposal.

5.2–8.0s:
M5 reviews the proposal and performs the FINAL approval.
Only after M5 approves, the next-month plan becomes confirmed.

END on:
confirmed next-month budget
+
M5 as final decision-maker
+
M2 as supporting AI coach.
```
