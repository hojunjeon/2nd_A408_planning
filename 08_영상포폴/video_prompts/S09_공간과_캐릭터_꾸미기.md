# S09 — 공간 / 캐릭터 꾸미기 | 8초 영상 생성 프롬프트



```text
@M5_keyfin_avatar
@M4_keyfin_room
@KeyFin_S09_reference

REFERENCE MAP

REF-A = M5_keyfin_avatar
REF-B = M4_keyfin_room
REF-C = KeyFin_S09_reference

Create ONE continuous 8-second stylized 3D KeyFin-world video in 16:9.

TIMING:

SHOT A = 0.0–2.4 seconds
SHOT B = 2.4–5.2 seconds
SHOT C = 5.2–8.0 seconds

CORE GOAL:

SHOT A:
M5 selects customization items.

SHOT B:
show the SAME KeyFin Room changing ONLY ONCE from default to customized,
then keep the customized room completely unchanged.
At the very end of SHOT B, the customized M5 appears inside the already-customized room.

SHOT C:
continue directly from that exact final frame.
Show the customized M5 clearly.
The room must NOT change again.

The room architecture, camera axis and major furniture must always remain REF-B.

==================================================
[0.0–2.4s]
SHOT A — SELECT CUSTOMIZATION ITEMS
==================================================

START FRAME

M5 is inside the stylized KeyFin world,
browsing a clean customization shop.

Use the exact M5 identity from REF-A.

CAMERA

Stable medium shot.
Very subtle push-in only.

SHOP UI

Show a small clean selection of customization items:
- framed wall artwork
- rug
- sofa cushion
- decorative plant or lamp
- avatar accessory / outfit item

Avoid dense text and prices.

ACTION

M5 chooses a small customization set.

Use 3–4 simple selection highlights/checks.

The viewer should understand:
“these selected items will now be applied.”

Do not transform the room during SHOT A.

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[2.4–5.2s]
SHOT B — EXACTLY ONE ROOM TRANSFORMATION
==================================================

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE:

THE ROOM MAY CHANGE EXACTLY ONE TIME IN SHOT B.

ONE transformation event only.

After the transformation is complete,
the room must remain visually identical for the rest of SHOT B and all of SHOT C.

Do NOT perform:
- a second transformation
- a second before/after
- another wipe
- another decoration change
- another room redesign
- another lighting transformation

==================================================
[2.4–3.45s]
BEFORE — DEFAULT REF-B ROOM
==================================================

Show the NORMAL DEFAULT KeyFin Room from REF-B.

IMPORTANT:

The BEFORE room must already contain the full normal architecture and furniture of REF-B.

Do NOT strip the room down to only a sofa and empty wall.

Preserve from REF-B:
- TV / media wall
- sofa
- desk
- shelves
- wall calendar
- window
- coffee table
- major plants
- lamps
- rug
- all major furniture positions

The BEFORE state is simply the default, uncustomized version of the SAME complete room.

It may have:
- default/plain wall art area
- default rug pattern
- default cushions
- default decorative objects

CAMERA

Use a LOCKED WIDE SHOT.

No camera movement.

No zoom.

No pan.

No reframing.

Do NOT display text such as:
“BEFORE”
“Before”
“AFTER”
“After”

No scene labels.
No captions.
No comparison words.

Hold this exact default room clearly until the single transformation moment.

==================================================
[3.45–3.70s]
THE ONLY ROOM CHANGE
==================================================

At approximately 3.45 seconds,
perform ONE clean customization transition.

Use ONE simple left-to-right wipe.

The wipe passes across the room ONCE.

As the wipe passes,
the default decor changes into the customized decor.

The room geometry and all major furniture remain perfectly aligned.

ONLY DECORATION changes.

Required visible customization differences:

1. WALL ART
A clear colorful framed artwork appears on a wall area.

2. RUG
The same rug area changes to a visibly different purple-blue patterned rug.

3. SOFA CUSHIONS
2–3 cushions change to more personalized colors/patterns.

4. DECOR ACCENT
One plant, lamp, shelf ornament, or table decoration visibly changes.

5. OPTIONAL SMALL DETAILS
A few books or small character-world decor items may be added.

IMPORTANT:

This transition happens ONCE.

When the wipe reaches the right edge,
the customization is FINISHED.

NO additional room change after this moment.

NO morphing.
NO room rebuild.
NO furniture teleportation.
NO second wipe.
NO second lighting change.
NO new architecture.

==================================================
[3.70–4.65s]
AFTER — HOLD THE CUSTOMIZED ROOM
==================================================

The room is now fully customized.

Hold the EXACT SAME customized room.

CAMERA REMAINS LOCKED.

Do not change:
- camera angle
- focal length
- room framing
- architecture
- furniture positions
- decor configuration
- lighting setup

Give the viewer enough time to clearly register the customized result.

The difference from BEFORE should come from decor only,
not from changing the entire room.

==================================================
[4.65–5.20s]
CUSTOMIZED M5 APPEARS AT THE END OF SHOT B
==================================================

The customized room MUST remain exactly unchanged.

Now introduce M5 into the SAME locked wide frame.

M5 must already be wearing the customized outfit/accessories selected in SHOT A.

M5 should appear naturally from one side of the existing room,
for example taking one or two small steps into the frame from beside the sofa or desk area.

Do NOT make M5 magically spawn.

Do NOT transform M5 in front of the camera here.

M5 is already customized when he appears.

M5 should match REF-A exactly in:
- face
- hairstyle
- eye style
- body proportions
- adult stylized identity

Only clothing/accessories are customized.

Possible customized items:
- cap
- jacket/accent layer
- headphones
- small crossbody accessory

M5 enters,
stops naturally,
and gives one small closed-mouth confident smile.

IMPORTANT:

THE ROOM DOES NOT CHANGE WHEN M5 APPEARS.

No new decoration appears with M5.

No second transformation.

No camera cut to another version of the room.

END SHOT B on:
CUSTOMIZED ROOM + CUSTOMIZED M5 together.

==================================================
[5.2–8.0s]
SHOT C — CUSTOMIZED M5 HERO SHOT, SAME ROOM
==================================================

SHOT C must continue directly from the final state of SHOT B.

START FRAME

The room must be the EXACT SAME customized room from the final frame of SHOT B.

No new room transformation.

No decor change.

No furniture change.

No lighting change.

No second before/after effect.

CAMERA

A gentle cut to a slightly closer medium-full composition of M5 is allowed,
BUT the background room must clearly remain the exact same customized room.

Do not cut to a differently designed room.

Do not switch to another camera angle that makes the room look different.

Prefer a simple controlled push-in from the existing axis.

M5

Show the customized M5 clearly.

Preserve:
- exact face
- exact hairstyle
- exact adult stylized proportions
- exact M5 identity

Customization may include:
- cap
- jacket/accent layer
- headphones
- small bag/accessory

M5 performs one restrained final pose:
- small closed-mouth smile
- slight posture adjustment
- optional small peace sign if natural

No jumping.
No exaggerated celebration.
No mouth animation.
No speaking.

END FRAME

Hold:
the same customized M4 room
+
the same customized M5.

The visual message should be immediately clear:

“I used my rewards to customize both my KeyFin room and my avatar.”

==================================================
STRICT FAILURE CONDITIONS
==================================================

THE RESULT IS WRONG IF:

- the room changes more than once
- there are two wipes
- there are two before/after transformations
- the room changes again when M5 appears
- the room changes again in SHOT C
- the camera cut makes SHOT C look like a new room
- major furniture moves
- the room architecture changes
- the BEFORE room is artificially empty or stripped down
- the AFTER room is a completely different environment
- lighting alone creates the before/after difference

- “BEFORE” or “AFTER” text appears
- captions appear
- scene labels appear
- subtitles appear

- M5 magically spawns
- M5 changes face
- M5 changes hairstyle
- M5 becomes a different character
- M5 becomes photorealistic
- M5 changes outfit twice

==================================================
EXACT RHYTHM
==================================================

0.0–2.4s:
M5 selects customization items.

2.4–3.45s:
locked wide shot of the COMPLETE default REF-B room.

3.45–3.70s:
ONE left-to-right wipe.
The room customization happens ONCE.

3.70–4.65s:
hold the customized room.
No further changes.

4.65–5.20s:
customized M5 naturally enters the SAME customized room.
The room remains unchanged.

5.20–8.0s:
continue with the SAME customized room and SAME customized M5.
Show M5 more clearly.
No second room transformation.
```
