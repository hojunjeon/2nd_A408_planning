# S11 — 예정지출 선제 관리 | 8초 영상 생성 프롬프트



```text
@M5_keyfin_avatar
@M2_ai_cat
@M4_keyfin_room
@KeyFin_S11_reference

REFERENCE MAP

REF-A = M5_keyfin_avatar
REF-B = M2_ai_cat
REF-C = M4_keyfin_room
REF-D = KeyFin_S11_reference

Create ONE continuous 8-second stylized 3D KeyFin-world video in 16:9.

Maintain the exact stylized 3D visual language of REF-C.
Preserve the exact identities of REF-A and REF-B.

CASTING LOCK:
ONLY M5 and M2 may appear.
NO real-world Yoon.
NO realistic human.
NO third character.
NO outside human hands.

==================================================
CORE STORY — KEEP IT SIMPLE AND MATCH REF-D
==================================================

SHOT A:
M5 and M2 check FIXED COSTS / UPCOMING PAYMENTS together.

SHOT B:
ONE upcoming payment is found to have insufficient balance.
M2 clearly points out the shortage and suggests a transfer.

SHOT C:
M5 presses ONE transfer/confirm button.
The transfer is executed ONCE.
The shortage disappears and the payment becomes ready.

This scene must be understood visually as:

UPCOMING PAYMENT CHECK
→ SHORTAGE FOUND
→ TRANSFER SUGGESTION
→ USER PRESSES BUTTON
→ PAYMENT READY

Do NOT add extra account-management logic.
Do NOT introduce many accounts.
Do NOT create a complicated financial flowchart.

Use REF-D as the visual grammar:
A = upcoming-payment overview
B = shortage / adjustment proposal
C = confirmed / updated state

TIMING:
SHOT A = 0.0–2.4 seconds
SHOT B = 2.4–5.2 seconds
SHOT C = 5.2–8.0 seconds

==================================================
[0.0–2.4s]
SHOT A — CHECK FIXED COSTS / UPCOMING PAYMENTS
==================================================

PURPOSE:
Show that KeyFin knows what fixed expenses and scheduled payments are coming soon.

START FRAME

M5 and M2 are together in the KeyFin Room.

They are looking at ONE clean floating UPCOMING PAYMENTS panel.

CAMERA

Stable medium-wide two-shot.
Very subtle push-in only.

Do not move the camera around the room.

==================================================
UI — MATCH THE SIMPLE REF-D STRUCTURE
==================================================

Show 3–4 upcoming payment items only.

Use clear icon-based rows/cards such as:

- HOME / RENT icon
- FOOD / LIVING icon
- CARD / BILL icon
- SUBSCRIPTION icon

Each item should have:
- one date/calendar cue
- one short horizontal amount/progress bar
- one simple status indicator

The layout should feel like REF-D:
clean, compact, easy to scan.

Do NOT show:
- account-transfer arrows yet
- source-account cards
- shortage warning yet
- red alert yet
- complex comparison graphs
- detailed account numbers

M5 ACTION

M5 scans the upcoming-payment list.

M2 looks at the same list.

M2 may make one small paw gesture toward the panel.

END FRAME

Hold the clean list long enough to understand:
“these fixed costs / payments are coming soon.”

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[2.4–5.2s]
SHOT B — ONE PAYMENT HAS INSUFFICIENT BALANCE + M2 SUGGESTS TRANSFER
==================================================

PURPOSE:
Focus on ONE shortage only.

Do not keep all 3–4 payments equally visible.

START FRAME

Select ONE upcoming payment from SHOT A.

For example:
one card bill / fixed-payment item.

The selected payment becomes visually prominent.

CAMERA

Stable medium composition.

Keep M5 and M2 visible,
but give the selected payment card enough space to read visually.

==================================================
SHORTAGE UI — VERY SIMPLE
==================================================

Show only three essential elements:

1. UPCOMING PAYMENT REQUIRED
2. CURRENT BALANCE FOR THAT PAYMENT
3. SHORTAGE

Use a direct visual comparison.

Example visual logic:

PAYMENT NEEDED
[██████████]

CURRENT BALANCE
[██████----]

SHORTAGE
the missing portion is highlighted RED

The red shortage must be obvious.

Do NOT use:
- stock chart
- generic “current vs expected” graph
- many bars with no meaning
- several accounts
- multiple warnings

==================================================
M2 TRANSFER SUGGESTION
==================================================

After the shortage is visible,
M2 points to the red shortage.

Then show ONE simple transfer suggestion card.

The suggestion card should visually communicate:

“move the missing amount here”

Use:
- one small transfer arrow
- one shortage amount placeholder
- one clear transfer/confirm button

Do NOT show a full banking transfer screen.

Do NOT show multiple source accounts.

Do NOT show complicated routing.

The proposal must feel like:
“there is a shortage; transfer just enough to cover it.”

M2 ACTION

M2 points to:
1. the shortage
2. the transfer suggestion

M2 does NOT press the button.

M5 ACTION

M5 looks at the shortage,
then looks at the transfer suggestion.

M5 does NOT approve yet.

END FRAME

Hold the proposal state clearly:

selected payment
+
red shortage
+
M2 transfer suggestion
+
one transfer/confirm button waiting for M5

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[5.2–8.0s]
SHOT C — M5 PRESSES BUTTON → TRANSFER DONE → PAYMENT READY
==================================================

PURPOSE:
Show one clear user action and one clear result.

START FRAME

Carry over the SAME shortage and SAME transfer suggestion from SHOT B.

Do NOT introduce another payment.
Do NOT change the shortage amount.
Do NOT create another proposal.

CAMERA

Stable medium composition.
Slightly favor M5 and the button.

==================================================
M5 ACTION — ONE BUTTON PRESS
==================================================

M5 visually checks the suggestion.

Then M5 performs ONE clear action:

M5 presses the single transfer/confirm button.

Only M5 performs the final action.

M2 does NOT press the button.

==================================================
TRANSFER RESULT — SIMPLE, ONE TIME ONLY
==================================================

ONLY AFTER M5 presses the button:

show ONE short transfer animation.

Keep it minimal:
- a small amount indicator moves once
OR
- the balance bar fills once

Do NOT show money flying around the room.
Do NOT repeat the transfer.

The red shortage disappears.

The current-balance bar becomes sufficient for the upcoming payment.

==================================================
PAYMENT READY STATE
==================================================

After the shortage disappears:

the selected upcoming-payment card receives ONE green check / ready state.

The final UI should visually match the spirit of REF-D's confirmed state:
simple, clean, green confirmation.

Do NOT show:
- multiple giant checks
- celebration effects
- coins
- confetti
- extra text

M5 ACTION

M5 gives one small satisfied reaction.

M2 gives one subtle approving reaction.

No cheering.
No jumping.
No exaggerated open-mouth laughter.

END FRAME

Hold a clean final state:

UPCOMING PAYMENT
+
SUFFICIENT BALANCE
+
ONE GREEN CHECK
+
M5 as the person who pressed the button
+
M2 as the AI coach who suggested the action

==================================================
UI STATE LOCK
==================================================

SHOT A:
simple upcoming fixed-cost/payment list.

SHOT B:
ONE selected payment
+
clear red shortage
+
simple M2 transfer suggestion
+
one waiting button.

SHOT C:
SAME selected payment
+
M5 presses the SAME button
+
shortage disappears
+
one green ready/check state.

Do NOT create a completely different UI between B and C.

==================================================
ROLE LOCK
==================================================

M2:
- helps identify the shortage
- suggests the transfer
- points to the action

M5:
- reviews the suggestion
- presses the final button
- authorizes execution

M2 never authorizes the transfer.

==================================================
STRICT FAILURE CONDITIONS
==================================================

THE RESULT IS WRONG IF:

- SHOT A is cluttered with account-transfer logic
- SHOT A already shows the shortage warning

- SHOT B shows many accounts
- SHOT B becomes a complex flowchart
- the shortage is not visually obvious
- there is no red missing portion
- M2's suggested action is unclear
- there is no single clear transfer/confirm button

- M2 presses the button
- transfer occurs before M5 presses
- M5 does not visibly press the button
- more than one transfer occurs
- the UI changes into a completely different screen in SHOT C
- a different payment is introduced
- final green check appears before the transfer completes

GLOBAL:
NO real-world Yoon.
NO third character.
NO photorealistic rendering.
NO room redesign.
NO dense readable text.
NO captions.
NO subtitles.
NO scene labels.
NO stock-market chart.
NO coins.
NO reward animation.

==================================================
EXACT 8-SECOND RHYTHM
==================================================

0.0–2.4s:
M5 + M2 check a simple fixed-cost / upcoming-payment list.

2.4–5.2s:
ONE payment is selected.
Its required amount is larger than the current balance.
The missing part is clearly RED.
M2 points out the shortage and shows ONE simple transfer suggestion with ONE button.

5.2–8.0s:
M5 presses that ONE button.
The transfer executes once.
The shortage disappears.
The selected upcoming payment receives ONE green ready/check state.

END CLEAN AND STABLE.
NO END CAPTION.
NO END MESSAGE.
```
