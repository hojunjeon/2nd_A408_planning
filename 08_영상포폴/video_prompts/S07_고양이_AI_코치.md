# S07 — 고양이 AI 코치 | 8초 영상 생성 프롬프트



```text
@M5_keyfin_avatar

REFERENCE MAP
REF-A = M5_keyfin_avatar
REF-B = M2_ai_cat
REF-C = M4_keyfin_room
REF-D = KeyFin_S07_reference

Create ONE continuous 8-second stylized 3D KeyFin-world video in 16:9.

GOAL:
M5 has a financial question → M2 AI cat naturally appears from behind the room wall/corner → user data forms a personalized coaching context → M2 provides a tailored practical answer.

M2 is a financial AI coach inside the KeyFin world, not a random mascot.

Maintain the exact stylized 3D visual language of REF-C throughout the entire scene.
M5 and M2 must preserve their exact identities from REF-A and REF-B.

==================================================
[0.0–2.2s]
SHOT A — M5 HAS A QUESTION, THEN M2 NATURALLY APPEARS
==================================================

START FRAME

Begin with M5 alone in the KeyFin Room.

M2 must NOT already be visible in the first frame.

Use REF-C as the exact room layout.

Place M5 naturally in the living area, slightly left or center-left of frame.

A wall edge, doorway edge, shelf partition, or architectural corner from the existing M4 room should be visible on the opposite side of the frame.

Do NOT invent a new wall or redesign the room.

CAMERA

Use a stable medium-wide shot.

No hard camera movement.
No orbit.
No sudden zoom.
A very subtle push-in is allowed.

M5 ACTION

M5 is thinking about a financial question.

Use a restrained thoughtful pose:
- one hand near chin
- slight pause
- eyes focused on a small question visual

QUESTION VISUAL

Keep the question visual minimal.

Use only a small cluster of simple icons near M5:
- housing
- food
- transport
- shopping
- optional game-console icon
- one question mark

Do not use readable sentences.
Do not create a large dashboard.

M2 ENTRANCE — IMPORTANT

M2 must enter the scene naturally from behind the existing wall/corner.

Timing suggestion:

0.0–0.8s:
M5 is alone and thinking.
M2 is completely hidden.

0.8–1.3s:
M2's head slowly peeks out from behind the wall/corner.
Only part of M2 is visible at first.

1.3–1.8s:
M2 steps or walks gently out from behind the wall into the room.

1.8–2.2s:
M2 stops near M5 at a comfortable distance.
M5 notices M2 and turns his gaze toward the cat.

M2 should feel like:
the familiar AI coach is coming to help after noticing M5's concern.

M2 must NOT:
- suddenly spawn
- teleport
- pop into existence
- fall from above
- appear through a portal
- appear with magical particles
- jump aggressively into frame

M2 MOVEMENT

Use small natural cat-like movement:
- gentle steps
- slight tail movement
- attentive posture

M2 should remain calm and competent.

No exaggerated mascot behavior.

M2 must NOT talk or move its mouth as if speaking.

END FRAME

End SHOT A with:
- M5 and M2 both visible
- M5 looking toward M2
- M2 looking attentively back at M5
- clear eye-line connection

The viewer should understand:
“M5 had a question, and the AI coach came over to help.”

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[2.2–5.0s]
SHOT B — USER DATA FORMS PERSONALIZED COACHING CONTEXT
==================================================

START FRAME

M2 is now naturally present from SHOT A.

Do not reintroduce M2.
Do not make M2 enter again.

CAMERA

Stable medium-wide composition.

Place M2 central or slightly off-center.
M5 remains visible but secondary.

DATA CARDS

Introduce only 4–5 clean data cards:

- budget
- transaction history
- spending pattern
- upcoming payments
- remaining period

MOTION

The data cards appear sequentially and organize into a clean semicircle or structured layout around M2.

Use controlled, readable movement.

Subtle connecting lines may converge toward one central coaching indicator.

Do NOT make the cards explode into frame.

Do NOT create excessive floating UI.

STYLE

Elegant premium fintech visualization.

Use subtle purple-blue glow consistent with REF-C.

No particle storm.
No sci-fi energy core.
No holographic explosion.
No dense unreadable text.
No long chatbot messages.

M2 ACTION

M2 remains calm and competent.

A small paw gesture toward the organized data is allowed.

M2 must not act comedic or overly cute.

M5 ACTION

M5 watches the data structure form.

Keep M5 reaction subtle and attentive.

END FRAME

Finish with a stable organized coaching-data structure around M2.

The viewer should understand:
“KeyFin uses my own financial data as the basis for coaching.”

HARD CUT.

==================================================
[5.0–8.0s]
SHOT C — TAILORED PRACTICAL ANSWER
==================================================

START FRAME

Return to a clean M5 + M2 two-shot.

Both characters are already established in the room.

CAMERA

Stable medium two-shot.

No dramatic movement.

ANSWER UI

Show one clean recommendation panel with 3–4 simple recommendation/status blocks.

Examples:
- safe
- caution
- adjust
- on track

Use:
- icons
- short bars
- checks
- simple status colors

Do NOT show:
- giant prohibition sign
- long readable chatbot text
- dense dashboard
- huge warning graphics

ACTION

M2 gently indicates one recommendation with a small paw gesture.

M5 reads the recommendation.

M5 gives a small nod of understanding.

M5 should look more confident after seeing the personalized guidance.

TONE

Supportive.
Practical.
Personalized.
Non-judgmental.

M2 is helping M5 make a decision,
not ordering M5 what to do.

END FRAME

Hold a stable two-shot of M5 and M2 with the recommendation visible.

The viewer should understand:
“the AI coach gives advice tailored to my financial situation.”
```
